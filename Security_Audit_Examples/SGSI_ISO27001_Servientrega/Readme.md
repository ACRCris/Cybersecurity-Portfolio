# Activity Summary

Portfolio Activity: **Construction of an ISMS under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (SGSI)**, from Module 4 of the BeTek / MAKAIA Cybersecurity Bootcamp. *Team activity — my role was documentation reviewer.*

Development of the baseline documentation for an Information Security Management System (ISMS) for a real, regulated company — **Servientrega S.A.** (logistics and transport, 10,000+ employees) — following **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**.

## Objectives accomplished

- **Context and scope (Clause 4)**: organizational context, internal/external factors, interested parties (customers, regulators — MinTIC, Supertransporte, SIC — employees, suppliers), scope boundaries, and **explicit exclusions**.
- **Asset inventory**: 15 assets classified by type (Information, Software, Hardware, Service, Person), owner, and location, each valued independently for **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability** to derive criticality.
- **Risk matrix**: 20 risk scenarios, each with threat, vulnerability, probability, affected CIA component, and a **treatment decision using all four ISO 27005 options** — mitigate, avoid, transfer, and accept.
- **Information security policy**: objective, scope, five differentiated roles, twelve general rules, a graded compliance regime, and review triggers (annual or event-driven).
- **Statement of Applicability (Annex A)**: 15 applicable controls with justification and associated risk, **10 controls declared not applicable with justification**, and three fully detailed procedures (secure authentication A.8.5, malware protection A.8.7, backups A.8.13) with assigned owner and review frequency.

## Folder Structure and Status

- `SGSI_ISO27001_Documentation.pdf`: consolidated 11-page ISMS documentation covering context, scope, asset inventory, risk matrix, policy, and Statement of Applicability.

## Note

This was a team deliverable; the documents were signed under a three-role approval flow (prepared / reviewed / approved), with my role being **document reviewer** (Consultor de Seguridad de la Información).

## Frameworks

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (ISMS, Annex A, Statement of Applicability); ISO/IEC 27005 (risk treatment); Colombian Law 1581 (data protection).
